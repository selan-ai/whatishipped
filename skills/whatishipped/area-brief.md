## How to plug in
- `window.Catalog.push({ group, items: [{ name, source, desc, demos: [Frame.d(label, () => html, wide)] }] })`.
  `source` is the file the type is defined in (`Features/Player/PlayerButton.swift:12`): the brandbook links it to
  the repo. `desc` is a few words on what it is. `wide` makes a demo stand on the screen ground at
  phone width; `'bleed'` runs it to the phone's edges.
- `window.Screens.push({ id, order, feature, name, source, desc, opens, states: [{ name, note, app, render: () =>
  Frame.phone({ nav, body, tabs, bottom, toast, sheet, over, banner, scroll, style, turned }) }] })`;
    on web, `render: () => Frame.web({ body, style })`.
  - `app` (on a state): the screenshot of the real app that shows it, or leave it out when none does.
  - `opens`: every way this screen opens another, read from its code:
    `[{ to: '<screen id>', how: 'push'|'sheet'|'modal'|'tab'|'replace'|'deeplink'|'external', from: '<element or state>', source: '<file:line>' }]`.
    `from` is what the person taps, in words; `source` is the one call that opens it, kept for checking and not
    shown. Use RECON's ids, including for screens other areas draw.
- `window.Icons.add(name, sourceName, '<path .../>', paint)` adds a missing glyph (paint: false outline, true filled,
  `'as-is'` multicolour). A screen with its own scrim sets `style: '--scrim: <colour>'`; a different foot clearance
  is `style: '--body-bottom: <px>'`; a state scrolled down passes `scroll: <points>`.

## Verify by looking and by measuring
The brandbook is served at <URL> (if not, run `node <OUT>/server.ts <PORT> &`). Check every screen and group you drew:

you could still change, only speckle on text; or until what still differs is the screenshot account's content
(titles, covers, dates, avatars), which the state's note then says. Other areas are being drawn at the same time;
ignore their problems.
